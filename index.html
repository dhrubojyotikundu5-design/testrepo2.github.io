How this was computed

Each of the forest's 300 decision trees votes a probability from the site conditions above. This page averages all 300 votes entirely in your browser — no server, no data leaves your machine.

Trained on the NE 7-states district & locality environmental dataset. High risk = Slide or Fall movement type; Low risk = Flow, Subsidence, Topple, or Composite. This is a statistical estimate from historical records, not a geotechnical assessment — it does not replace a site survey by a qualified engineer.
